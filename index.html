// 05 — WHY TRUST THE ROLL?

The Dice doesn't
pick favourites.

The experience is simple on the surface, but the selection process is designed so that the result isn't secretly being influenced by the animation or by someone's preference.

Fair selection

Each available choice is given an equal opportunity to be selected. The result is determined before the visual roll settles.

Transparent

The outcome and decision information can be recorded so there is a clear result rather than a hidden choice.

Simple on purpose

You don't need to understand the machinery behind the roll. You just need to agree on the choices and let the Dice decide.

Under the hood FOR THOSE WHO WANT TO KNOW 
selection = secureRandom(availableChoices);

result = selection;

visualDice = settleTo(result);

recordDecision({
  result,
  timestamp,
  decisionId
});
